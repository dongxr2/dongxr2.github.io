AI-assisted Classroom Observation

Analyzes classroom video using deep learning and distributed computing to generate class, student and teacher behavior reports.

Method & application

Processes classroom streams in rotation, produces segment reports, and aggregates them according to class schedules. The commissioning institution is anonymized in the source.
