Remote sensing data

Multi-source Remote Sensing Data Fusion

Unifies remote sensing data with different spatial resolutions and semantic labels into an integrated geographic dataset.

Method & application

Uses coordinate-offset estimation, resampling and pixel-level alignment to integrate data or segmentation results from Google Map, Tianditu, GID and GlobeLand30.
