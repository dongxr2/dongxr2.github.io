Remote sensing & navigation

Navigation Planning from Optical Remote Sensing

Combines road extraction, land-cover classification and A* search in a unified cost map for emergency routing across complex terrain.

Method & application

HC-RoadNet extracts road networks and NX-SegNet classifies land cover. Road connectivity and terrain traversability are fused before searching between the specified endpoints.
