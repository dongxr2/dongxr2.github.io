Long-term Drift Compensation for Gas Sensor Arrays

Studies incremental domain-adversarial and online learning methods for data drift in long-running sensors.

Method & application

Organizes real-time sample streams using a pseudo-label control mechanism for drift compensation in long-term detection of gases such as ethanol and toluene.
